Premium Coffee from World-Renowned Origins

East Africa

Ethiopia (Yirgacheffe, Sidamo, Guji) and Kenya AA — renowned for bright acidity, complex floral, blueberry, and citrus aromas.

Latin America

Colombia (Huila, Antioquia), Guatemala (Antigua), Costa Rica (Tarrazú) — balanced sweetness with caramel, nutty and chocolate notes.

Brazil & South America

Brazil (Minas Gerais, Santos) offers smooth chocolate and hazelnut profiles. Bolivia and Peru provide unique high-altitude character.

Asia Pacific

Sumatra Mandheling & Gayo (Indonesia), Vietnam, and Papua New Guinea — full-bodied, earthy and spicy with distinctive herbal notes.

Jamaica & Hawaii

Jamaica Blue Mountain — world-famous mild, smooth and balanced. Hawaii Kona offers rare and luxurious flavor profiles.

Specialty Micro-Lots

Geisha (Panama), rare Ethiopian heirloom varieties, and limited competition-winning coffees for the most discerning connoisseurs.

Fine Wines from Iconic Regions

France

Bordeaux Classified Growths, Burgundy Grand Cru, Rhône Valley, Champagne, and Loire Valley — the benchmark of elegance and complexity.

Italy

Tuscany (Brunello, Chianti, Super Tuscans), Piedmont (Barolo, Barbaresco), Veneto (Amarone), and Sicily — passionate and diverse.

Spain & Portugal

Rioja, Ribera del Duero, Priorat, and Portuguese Port & Douro Valley — rich, structured wines with exceptional aging potential.

New World Classics

Argentina Malbec (Mendoza & Uco Valley), Australian Shiraz & Cabernet, Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon, and New Zealand Pinot Noir.

Emerging Regions

South Africa (Stellenbosch), Chile (Maipo & Colchagua), and Lebanon (Bekaa Valley) — offering outstanding quality and value.

Specialty & Prestige

About Vertex Global Ltd
